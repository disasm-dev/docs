disasm.dev solves anti-bot challenges over plain HTTP. You send us what the challenge gave you, we send back a solution,
and you submit it from your own client. There is no browser to run.

## What you can solve

| Product | Challenges | Base URL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [DataDome](/datadome/overview/) | Interstitial, slider and audio CAPTCHA, tags | `https://dd.antibotapi.com` |
| [Incapsula](/incapsula/reese84/) | reese84 (with proof of work), utmvc | `https://incap.antibotapi.com` |
| [Kasada](/kasada/overview/) (beta) | `/tl` payload, `x-kpsdk-cd` proof of work | `https://ksd.antibotapi.com` |
| [Ticketmaster](/ticketmaster/tmpt/) | `tmpt` cookie | `https://tmpt.antibotapi.com` |
| [Amazon](/amazon/metadata1/) | Sign-in `metadata1` | `https://metadata1.antibotapi.com` |

## How a solve works

1. You make the request that gets blocked, and read the values the challenge gives you.
2. You send those values to us and get back a payload.
3. You submit the payload from the same session and keep the cookie or token it earns.

We only compute. The requests to the site, the proxy and the cookie jar stay yours, so your client has to look like a
real Chrome browser. [Match a real browser](/start/fingerprinting/) covers that.
